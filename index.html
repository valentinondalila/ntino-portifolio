A little about me

I think good technology should feel thoughtful, not complicated.

I’m Valentino, a software engineer who enjoys turning a jumble of ideas into something clear and useful. I like the puzzle of making things work; I like it even more when the result feels natural to the person using it.

That means sweating the small stuff, staying curious, and remembering there’s always a human on the other side of the screen. Still learning, still building, and always happy to talk through a good idea.
